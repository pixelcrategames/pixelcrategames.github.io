Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers PixelCrate Poster, a private tool run by Pixel Crate Games.

What the tool does

PixelCrate Poster uploads videos made by Pixel Crate Games to the Pixel Crate Games accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. Only Pixel Crate Games uses it.

What data it uses

It only connects to Pixel Crate Games' own accounts. It uses the access those platforms grant (such as permission to upload videos and read basic account info) to post our videos. It does not collect, store, sell or share data about anyone else, and it has no public sign-up.

Where data is kept

Account access tokens and our video files are stored on our own private server and are used only to post our videos.
